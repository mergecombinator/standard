# Merge Role Taxonomy

**Version 1.1**

Reference for the Pursue and Form Agreements. Each participant in a venture
holds one of the roles defined here. Parties select a role rather than define a
new one.

## The six roles

### 1. Problem Owner
The operator or mission stakeholder who validates that the gap is real.

- **Owns:** the requirement, the reality check, access to actual users.
- **Commits:** episodic time. Reviews, honest feedback, introductions where permitted.
- **Receives:** no equity or compensation by default. See the government ethics section below.
- **Governance:** none.
- **Purpose:** standard startup frameworks do not define this role. In defense
  ventures, access to the holder of the requirement determines whether the
  product meets an operational need.

### 2. Technical Founder
Owns the product and the architecture. Builds the thing.

- **Owns:** technology decisions, the build, technical hiring.
- **Commits:** full-time, or a written date by which they go full-time.
- **Receives:** the largest founder position.
- **Governance:** board seat at formation.

### 3. Venture Lead
Owns the business. Formation, capital, contracts, hiring, the P&L.

- **Owns:** company formation, capital, contracts, hiring, and financial performance.
- **Commits:** full-time by formation. May be held on a part-time basis before then.
- **Receives:** a founder position, sized according to when the holder commits full-time.
- **Governance:** board seat at formation.

### 4. Studio
The contracting vehicle and the team assembler. Merge Combinator holds this
role in its own ventures.

- **Owns:** the contracting vehicle and CAGE code, program and operator
  relationships, the acquisition path, back-office functions, and any unfilled
  seat, held on a part-time basis until a permanent holder is recruited.
- **Commits:** the above, plus any seat it holds on a part-time basis.
- **Receives:** an equity position under the Form Agreement. No fees, retainers,
  or billed hours.
- **Governance:** board seat at formation.

### 5. Contributor
Scoped, time-boxed work. An engineer, a designer, a proposal writer.

- **Owns:** a defined deliverable.
- **Commits:** a defined scope and end date.
- **Receives:** cash, a small equity position, or credit, stated at the time of
  engagement.
- **Governance:** none.

### 6. Advisor
Named, low time commitment, opens doors and reviews work.

- **Owns:** nothing operational.
- **Commits:** a few hours per quarter and the use of their name.
- **Receives:** a small equity position or nothing.
- **Governance:** none.

## Seats held on a part-time basis

The Venture Lead, the Technical Founder, or a named functional seat (Operations,
Product, or Technology) may be **held on a part-time basis**. This allows a
studio partner to fill a gap in the team without selling a consulting service.

A seat held on a part-time basis requires all three of the following, recorded
when the seat is taken. A seat without all three is a paid engagement, not a
part-time seat under this taxonomy.

1. **Term.** The seat is held through Pursue and does not continue on a
   part-time basis after formation.
2. **Conversion.** The position converts at Form. It does not convert to a
   monthly fee.
3. **Succession.** Recruiting the permanent holder of the seat is a named Form
   deliverable, owned by whoever is sitting in it.

**Reason for the conditions.** A monthly fee is easier to justify in any given
month than equity whose value is realized years later. Recording the term,
conversion, and succession at the outset keeps a part-time seat from becoming an
open-ended service arrangement.

## Government ethics

A current federal employee, including a Guard or Reserve member in a duty status,
generally cannot hold a financial interest in a company that sells to or is
regulated by their own organization. The relevant authorities are 18 U.S.C. § 208
and the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at
5 CFR 2635. Rules also vary by component and by the person's specific position.

**Default:** Problem Owner is a non-equity role. Do not offer equity, advisory
shares, options, or deferred compensation to a serving government stakeholder. If
a specific person seems like they should hold something, they clear it with their
ethics counselor first and the venture gets it in writing before anything is
issued.

The default protects the government stakeholder from a conflict of interest as
well as protecting the venture.

## Splits

Default equity positions by role are published in Schedule 2 of the Form
Agreement. The parties to a Form Agreement may record a different position, and
the reason for it, in its Schedule 1. Pursue assigns roles without assigning
equity.
