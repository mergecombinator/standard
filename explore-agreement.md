## 2. Review

**Review date: {{review_date}}** (the "Review Date")

On or about the Review Date, the Parties will meet to review the Deliverables
and decide jointly on one of the following outcomes:

- **Continue.** Proceed toward the Opportunity under a separate written
  agreement, such as a Pursue Agreement.
- **Redirect.** Evaluate a different opportunity under a new Explore Agreement
  or another written agreement.
- **Extend.** Extend this Agreement once, by a written amendment signed by both
  Parties, for an additional thirty days with newly named Deliverables. This
  Agreement may not be extended a second time.
- **Conclude.** Allow this Agreement to expire under Section 12.

## 3. Considerations for Continuing

Before entering into a separate agreement to continue, the Parties intend to
consider whether each of the following conditions has been met:

1. Each Party completed its Deliverable.
2. Demand for the Opportunity exists independently of the Parties. At least
   five prospective customers or users who are not affiliated with either Party
   have described the underlying problem, or a Party holds a written expression
   of interest, a letter of intent, or a funding commitment.
3. Each role required to continue has a named individual responsible for it
   through the term of the next agreement.

This Section 3 states the Parties' intentions only. It does not obligate either
Party to enter into any further agreement.

## 4. Confidentiality

"Confidential Information" means non-public business, technical, financial, or
other information that a Party (the "Discloser") discloses to the other Party
(the "Recipient") in connection with the Opportunity, in any form, that is
marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would
understand to be confidential from its nature and the circumstances of
disclosure.

The Recipient will (a) use Confidential Information only to evaluate the
Opportunity; (b) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its
own information of similar sensitivity, and no less than reasonable care; and
(c) disclose it only to its employees, officers, contractors, and professional
advisors who need to know it for that purpose and are bound by confidentiality
obligations at least as protective as this Section 4 (together,
"Representatives"). The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this
Section 4 by its Representatives.

These obligations do not apply to information that (a) is or becomes public
other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was known to the Recipient
without a duty of confidentiality before disclosure; (c) is independently
developed by the Recipient without use of the Confidential Information; or
(d) is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality. The
Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law,
regulation, or court order, provided that, where legally permitted, it gives
the Discloser prompt prior notice and reasonable cooperation in seeking
protective treatment.

The obligations in this Section 4 continue for one year after the Effective
Date. For Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under
applicable law, they continue for as long as the information remains a trade
secret. On the Discloser's written request, the Recipient will return or
destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except for copies retained
under a legal obligation or in routine backup systems, which remain subject to
this Section 4.

## 5. Intellectual Property

Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in the information,
materials, and intellectual property it brings to the evaluation and in each
Deliverable it produces. Nothing in this Agreement assigns, transfers, or
grants a license to any intellectual property, data, or other right, except
the limited right to use Confidential Information for the purpose stated in
Section 4. If the Parties intend to develop anything jointly, they will agree
in writing on its ownership and permitted use before beginning that work.

## 6. No Exclusivity

This Agreement is non-exclusive. Each Party remains free to pursue the
Opportunity or any other opportunity independently or with third parties, and
neither Party restricts whom the other may hire, engage, partner with, or sell
to, subject to Section 4.

## 7. Costs and Compensation

Neither Party owes the other any fee, compensation, equity, or reimbursement
under this Agreement. Each Party bears its own costs and expenses.

## 8. Relationship of the Parties

The Parties are independent contractors. This Agreement does not create a
partnership, joint venture, agency, employment, or fiduciary relationship.
Neither Party has authority to bind the other or to make any representation or
commitment on the other's behalf. Neither Party will use the other's name,
logo, or trademarks, or announce this Agreement publicly, without the other
Party's prior written consent.

## 9. Export Control and Government Information

Each Party will comply with applicable export control and sanctions laws,
including the International Traffic in Arms Regulations and the Export
Administration Regulations. A Discloser will identify in writing, before
disclosure, any information that is export controlled or that is Controlled
Unclassified Information, and will not disclose it unless the Recipient has
agreed in writing to receive it. No classified information will be disclosed
under this Agreement.

## 10. No Warranty

All information and Deliverables are provided "as is." Neither Party makes any
representation or warranty, express or implied, about the accuracy,
completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose of any information or
Deliverable it provides.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Except for a breach of Section 4 or Section 9, neither Party is liable to the
other under this Agreement for any indirect, incidental, special,
consequential, or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost business
opportunity.

## 12. Term, Termination, and Survival

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and expires on the Review Date
unless extended under Section 2. Either Party may terminate this Agreement
earlier, for any reason, by written notice to the other Party, effective on
delivery. Expiration or termination gives rise to no liability. Sections 4, 5,
and 8 through 13 survive expiration or termination.

## 13. General

**Future agreements.** Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to
negotiate or enter into any further agreement.

**Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the
Parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and
agreements concerning that subject matter.

**Amendment and waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written
instrument signed by both Parties. A failure or delay in enforcing any
provision is not a waiver of it.

**Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other
Party's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially
all of the assigning Party's business to which this Agreement relates.

**Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective
when delivered by email to the individual who signed this Agreement for the
receiving Party, or to another address that Party designates in writing.

**Equitable relief.** A breach of Section 4 may cause irreparable harm for
which damages would be an inadequate remedy, and the Discloser may seek
injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

**Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining
provisions remain in effect and the unenforceable provision will be enforced to
the maximum extent permitted.

**Governing law.** The laws of {{governing_state}} govern this Agreement,
without regard to its conflict of laws rules.
