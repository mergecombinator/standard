# Form Agreement

**Version 0.9**

*Pre-release draft of the third document of The Merge Standard, published by
Merge Combinator, LLC under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license. Not yet reviewed by counsel. Proposed changes are received at
github.com/mergecombinator/standard.*

*This Agreement grants equity in a company to a contributing partner, which
may be an individual or an organization, in exchange for defined contributions
of work, roles, relationships, or capabilities. It does not cover an
investment of money, which is agreed separately on standard financing
documents. The text of this Agreement is the same for every deal. The terms
that differ between deals are set out in Schedule 1.*

---

This Form Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective
Date stated in Schedule 1 between the company named in Schedule 1 (the
"Company") and the contributing partner named in Schedule 1 (the "Partner").
The Company and the Partner are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

Schedule 1 states whether the Company is an existing entity or is to be formed
under Schedule 3. If Schedule 3 applies, then until the Company is formed and
signs a joinder to this Agreement, the obligations of the Company are
obligations of the Founding Parties named in Schedule 3, acting jointly.

## 1. Definitions

"Contributions" means the work, roles, relationships, intellectual property
licenses, and other contributions the Partner will provide, as listed in
Schedule 1.

"Equity" means common stock if the Company is a corporation, or common units
or membership interests if the Company is a limited liability company.

"Fully Diluted Capitalization" means all outstanding Equity, plus all Equity
issuable on exercise or conversion of outstanding options, warrants,
convertible securities, and simple agreements for future equity, plus all
Equity reserved under any equity incentive plan, in each case as of the time
of measurement.

"Grant" means the Equity issued to the Partner under Section 2.

"Milestone" means each event listed in Schedule 1 on which a portion of the
Grant vests.

"Role" means a role defined in the Merge Role Taxonomy, version 1.1 or later.

"Change of Control" means a merger, consolidation, or sale of all or
substantially all of the Company's assets, or a transaction or series of
related transactions in which the holders of the Company's voting Equity
before the transaction hold less than a majority of the voting power after it.
A Financing is not a Change of Control.

"Financing" means a bona fide transaction or series of related transactions in
which the Company raises money from investors through the sale of Equity,
preferred equity, or convertible securities.

"Cause" means the Partner's (a) material breach of this Agreement that remains
uncured thirty days after written notice describing it; (b) fraud,
embezzlement, or willful misconduct that materially harms the Company; or
(c) conviction of, or plea of no contest to, a felony. If the Partner is an
organization, Cause also includes the acts in clauses (b) and (c) by the
individual the Partner designates to perform its Contributions, if the Partner
does not replace that individual within thirty days after written notice.

## 2. Grant

Within thirty days after the Effective Date, or after formation of the Company
if Schedule 3 applies, the Company will issue to the Partner Equity equal to
the percentage of Fully Diluted Capitalization stated in Schedule 1, measured
immediately after the issuance. The Grant is subject to vesting under
Section 3. The Partner will pay the purchase price stated in Schedule 1, which
is nominal unless Schedule 1 states otherwise.

The Grant is diluted proportionately with all other holders of Equity by any
later issuance of Equity. This Agreement gives the Partner no anti-dilution
protection.

The Company represents that the capitalization table attached to Schedule 1 is
complete and accurate as of the Effective Date, and that it has obtained every
approval of its board, managers, members, or stockholders required to issue the
Grant.

## 3. Vesting

**Milestone portion.** The portion of the Grant stated in Schedule 1 as the
milestone portion vests in the amounts assigned to each Milestone when that
Milestone is achieved, if it is achieved on or before its outside date.

**Time portion.** The portion of the Grant stated in Schedule 1 as the time
portion vests over the period and with the cliff stated in Schedule 1, in equal
monthly installments after the cliff, while the Partner continues to provide
its Contributions.

**Confirming a Milestone.** The Partner will notify the Company in writing when
it believes a Milestone has been achieved, with reasonable supporting evidence.
The Company will confirm or dispute the Milestone in writing within fifteen
business days. A Milestone the Company does not dispute within that period is
deemed achieved. A dispute is resolved under Section 12.

**Missed Milestones.** The portion of the Grant assigned to a Milestone that is
not achieved by its outside date is forfeited, unless the Parties agree in
writing to extend the outside date or to reassign that portion to a new
Milestone.

**Change of Control.** If a Change of Control occurs and, within twelve months
after it, the Company or its successor ends the Partner's relationship without
Cause, the entire unvested Grant vests immediately. Schedule 1 may provide
different acceleration.

## 4. Contributions

The Partner will provide the Contributions with reasonable skill and care, in
the Role and at the time commitment stated in Schedule 1. If the Partner is an
organization, it will designate in Schedule 1 the individual who will perform
its Contributions and may replace that individual with the Company's consent,
which the Company will not unreasonably withhold.

The Partner is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Company.
The Company owes the Partner no cash compensation for the Contributions unless
Schedule 1 states otherwise.

If the Partner holds a Role on a part-time basis, it will recruit, or help the
Company recruit, a permanent holder of that Role as stated in Schedule 1.

## 5. Ending the Relationship

Either Party may end the Partner's relationship with the Company on thirty
days' written notice, or immediately for Cause in the case of the Company.
When the relationship ends:

1. Vesting stops. Vested Equity remains the Partner's.
2. Unvested Equity is forfeited, or, if it has been issued, the Company may
   repurchase it within ninety days at the lower of the price the Partner paid
   for it and its fair market value.
3. Section 6, Section 9, and Sections 11 through 13 survive.

## 6. Intellectual Property

**Retained property.** Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in
the intellectual property it owned before the Effective Date or develops
outside the scope of the Contributions. Schedule 1 lists any pre-existing
intellectual property the Partner licenses to the Company and the terms of the
license.

**Work product.** Intellectual property the Partner creates in performing the
Contributions, solely or jointly, and that relates to the Company's business,
is assigned to the Company when it is created. The Partner will sign any
further documents reasonably needed to record that assignment. If the Partner
is an organization, it is responsible for obtaining the same assignment from
each individual who performs its Contributions.

**Schedule 3.** If the Company is formed under Schedule 3, each Founding Party
assigns or licenses to the Company the intellectual property listed for it in
Schedule 3, on formation, in exchange for its Equity.

## 7. Governance and Information

The Partner has the board, manager, or observer rights, if any, stated in
Schedule 1.

Until the Company's first Financing in which investors receive their own
information rights, the Company will deliver to the Partner, on request and no
more often than quarterly, its current capitalization table and its most
recent financial statements. The Partner will treat them as confidential under
Section 11.

If Schedule 1 grants a participation right, the Partner may purchase, in the
Company's next Financing, up to the portion of the securities offered that
equals its percentage of Fully Diluted Capitalization immediately before that
Financing, on the terms offered to the other investors.

## 8. Financing and Change of Control

The Company is intended to be able to complete a Financing without
restructuring. In a Financing or Change of Control approved by the Company's
board and by holders of a majority of its outstanding Equity, the Partner will
sign the voting, transfer, and other agreements that all other holders of
Equity of the same class are asked to sign, on the same terms as those
holders.

## 9. Transfer

The Partner may not transfer unvested Equity. Vested Equity may be transferred
only in compliance with the Company's governing documents and applicable
securities laws. A Partner that is an organization may transfer vested Equity
to an affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business.

## 10. Representations

Each Party represents that it has the authority to enter into and perform this
Agreement, and that doing so does not breach any other agreement it is bound
by.

The Partner represents that it is acquiring the Equity for its own account and
not with a view to distribution, that it understands the Equity has not been
registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or any state securities law and is
subject to restrictions on transfer, and that it can bear the economic risk of
the investment.

The Partner represents that no individual performing its Contributions is a
government employee who is prohibited by applicable ethics law from holding a
financial interest in the Company, and that it will notify the Company if that
changes.

## 11. Confidentiality and Compliance

Each Party will treat the other's non-public information under terms equivalent
to Section 4 of the Explore Agreement, version 1.1, published at
github.com/mergecombinator/standard, except that the obligations continue for
two years after the Partner's relationship with the Company ends, and for
trade secrets, for as long as they remain trade secrets.

Each Party will comply with applicable export control and sanctions laws,
including the International Traffic in Arms Regulations and the Export
Administration Regulations.

## 12. Disputes

The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute under this Agreement
through discussion between senior representatives for thirty days after either
Party gives written notice of the dispute. If the dispute is not resolved, the
Parties will attempt non-binding mediation for an additional thirty days
before either Party starts litigation. Either Party may seek injunctive relief
at any time to protect its intellectual property or confidential information.

## 13. General

**Tax.** Each Party is responsible for its own taxes. If the Grant is subject
to vesting and the Partner is an individual or other taxpayer eligible to do
so, the Partner may file an election under Section 83(b) of the Internal
Revenue Code within thirty days after issuance, and the Company will cooperate.
If the Company is taxed as a partnership, the Parties intend Equity issued
under this Agreement to qualify as a profits interest.

**Money.** This Agreement does not cover any investment of money in the
Company. Any such investment is agreed separately.

**Default terms.** Schedule 2 publishes default positions by Role. The Parties
may agree to a position different from the default by stating it, and the
reason for it, in Schedule 1. The text of this Agreement does not change.

**Entire agreement.** This Agreement, including its Schedules, is the entire
agreement between the Parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all
prior discussions and agreements concerning that subject matter, including any
Explore or Pursue Agreement, except for confidentiality obligations that
continue under those agreements.

**Amendment and waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written
instrument signed by both Parties. A failure or delay in enforcing any
provision is not a waiver of it.

**Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other
Party's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially
all of the assigning Party's business, or, for the Company, in a Change of
Control.

**Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective
when delivered by email to the individual who signed this Agreement for the
receiving Party, or to another address that Party designates in writing.

**Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining
provisions remain in effect and the unenforceable provision will be enforced to
the maximum extent permitted.

**Governing law and venue.** The laws of the state named in Schedule 1 govern
this Agreement, without regard to its conflict of laws rules, and the state
and federal courts located in that state have exclusive jurisdiction.

**Authority.** Each individual signing below represents that they are
authorized to sign this Agreement on behalf of the Party named above their
signature.

**Counterparts and electronic signatures.** This Agreement may be signed in
counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which has the same effect as
an original handwritten signature.

## Drafting notes

These notes are not part of the Agreement.

- **Status.** Version 0.9 is a pre-release draft. It has not been reviewed by
  counsel. The Schedule 2 percentages are proposed defaults and are to be
  confirmed by Merge Combinator before version 1.0.
- **What this document is for.** A SAFE standardized the terms on which an
  investor provides money before a priced round. This Agreement does the same
  for a partner who provides work, roles, or capabilities instead of money.
  The text stays fixed, and the variables live in Schedule 1, in the same way
  a SAFE fixes its text and varies its valuation cap and discount.
- **Why milestones.** A grant that vests on named milestones protects the
  Company if the Partner does not deliver, and the deemed-acceptance rule in
  Section 3 protects the Partner from a milestone being withheld without a
  stated reason.
- **Securities and tax.** Issuing Equity for services is a securities issuance
  and a taxable event. Counsel should confirm the exemption relied on and the
  tax treatment for each deal, particularly when the Partner is an
  organization or the Company is a limited liability company.
